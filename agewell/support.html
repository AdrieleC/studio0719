Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Every free feature works without one. An account (Apple, Google or email) is optional.
Where is my data?
On your iPhone. Meals, workouts, photos and Health data are not uploaded.
How do I cancel Premium?
Settings › your name › Subscriptions › AgeWell Premium › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
How do I restore Premium on a new iPhone?
Settings in AgeWell › Premium › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
How do I delete my account?
AgeWell › Settings › Account › Delete account. If you signed in with Apple, AgeWell also revokes its access to your Apple ID.
