Common questions

How do we pair?
One of you creates the Duet and sends the invite from the app. Your partner taps the iCloud link on their iPhone. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.
Can I use Duet alone?
You can set up goals and rewards while your partner's seat is open; the fun starts when they join.
Does Duet+ cover both of us?
Yes. One partner subscribes and both get Duet+.
How do we cancel Duet+?
The partner who subscribed: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Duet+ › Cancel Subscription.
How do we delete our data?
Duet › Profile › Leave Duet and delete our data.
