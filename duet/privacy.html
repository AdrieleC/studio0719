Duet: Couple Goals & Rewards — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Studio 0719 Inc. ("we", "us") makes Duet. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: your Duet data stays on your device or in your own iCloud, accounts are optional, and we never sell data, show ads or track you.

Data that stays on your device

The app stores your app settings and walkthrough progress on your iPhone. We don't receive it.

Your shared Duet data (iCloud)

Your couple's goals, completions, points, rewards, negotiations, profile names and profile photos are stored in Apple's iCloud using CloudKit — in the iCloud account of the partner who created the Duet, and shared only with the one partner they invite through a private iCloud share. Studio 0719 cannot see, read or access this data. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud. Profile photos are processed on your device before upload.

Deleting shared data

In Duet › Profile › Leave Duet and delete our data. If you created the Duet, this deletes the shared data from iCloud for both of you. If you joined, it removes you from the share and clears Duet on your device. You can also manage iCloud data in Settings › your name › iCloud.

Optional account

You can use the app without an account. If you create one (Sign in with Apple, Google, or email and password), we use Google Firebase Authentication to keep it. Firebase stores your email address, an account ID, the sign-in method, and — if Apple or Google shares it — your name. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we only see a private relay address. We use this information only to sign you in, send account emails you ask for (verification, password reset), and link purchases to your account. Firebase is operated by Google LLC; data may be processed in the United States.

Purchases

Subscriptions (Duet+ — yearly; one subscription covers both partners) are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To manage subscriptions and let premium follow your account across devices, we use RevenueCat, Inc., which receives your purchase history (product, dates, status) and an anonymous or account ID.

What we don't do

No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or renting of data, no profiling. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Keeping and deleting data

Delete your account any time in the app (Settings › Account › Delete account); this deletes it from Firebase and revokes Sign in with Apple. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for accounting and tax law. You can also email us to delete your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Québec's Law 25, Canada's PIPEDA, the EU/UK GDPR and U.S. state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information and to withdraw consent. Email hello@studio0719.com; we answer within 30 days. The person in charge of the protection of personal information at Studio 0719 Inc. is Adriele Clemente, reachable at the same address. You may also complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or your local privacy regulator.

Security

Account credentials are handled by Firebase and Apple; data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
