Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Create your character and play. An account is optional.
What's free?
Chapters 1 and 2 — your first 14 days. Chapter 3 onward needs Journey to Health Monthly.
How do I cancel?
Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Journey to Health › Cancel Subscription.
Do I lose progress if I cancel?
No. Your progress stays; locked chapters wait for you.
How do I delete my account?
Journey to Health › Settings › Account › Delete account.
